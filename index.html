About

Story is everything.

I'm Inba Kumar U — a freelance video editor based in Chennai. I believe that film editing is the invisible art: when done right, the audience never notices the cut, only the feeling.

From feature film end credits and Hotstar originals to BTS vlogs, trailers, and reels, I craft edits that serve the story — one precise cut at a time.

My goal: to become a professional film editor whose name you'll see in the credits of films that matter.
